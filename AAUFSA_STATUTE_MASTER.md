## SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.
This Act may be cited as the **"Automated Asset Utilization and Fiscal Solvency Act of 2026"** or the **"AAUFSA"**.

## SECTION 2. FINDINGS AND PURPOSE.
1. The rapid expansion of autonomous artificial intelligence, cyber-physical systems, and algorithmic orchestration transforms corporate productivity while structural human payroll bases experience compression.
2. To preserve the long-term fiscal solvency of the Social Security Trust Funds and finance the technical transition of the displaced workforce—without penalizing software innovation, Cloud/SaaS development, or small and medium-sized enterprises—it is necessary to establish a deterministic, auditable parity excise tax on permanent structural corporate displacement.

## SECTION 3. AMENDMENT OF INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1986 (CHAPTER 56).
Subtitle D of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 is amended by adding at the end thereof the following new chapter:

### CHAPTER 56 — AUTOMATED WORKFORCE DISPLACEMENT PARITY TAX

#### SEC. 5000F. IMPOSITION OF AUTOMATED WORKFORCE DISPLACEMENT TAX.
* **(a) In General:** There is hereby imposed on any applicable taxpayer that executes a structural automated displacement event an excise tax equal to **15.30 percent** (`1530 basis points`) of the **Effective Displaced Baseline Payroll** attributable to such event for the taxable year.
* **(b) Parity Principle:** The rate of 15.30 percent establishes exact fiscal parity with the combined employer and employee contributions under the Federal Insurance Contributions Act (FICA — 12.40% OASDI and 2.90% Hospital Insurance).

#### SEC. 5000G. SAFE HARBORS, EXEMPTIONS, AND RECAPTURE RULES.
* **(a) Employee Reassignment and Reskilling Safe Harbor (36-Month Rule):**
  1. **Exemption:** The tax imposed by Section 5000F shall be reduced to **$0.00 (Zero Dollars)** if the applicable taxpayer reassigns, retrains, or transitions the affected human employee to an internal role within the same business unit or controlled group at equal or greater total compensation for a continuous commitment period of not less than **36 months**.
* **(b) Small Business and Controlled Group Gross Receipts Threshold ($5,000,000.00 USD Rule):**
  1. **Exemption:** No tax shall be imposed under Section 5000F on any taxpayer whose consolidated annual gross receipts for the preceding taxable year do not exceed **$5,000,000.00 USD** (`500,000,000 cents`).
  2. **Controlled Group Aggregation:** For purposes of paragraph (1), all persons and corporate domains treated as a single employer under subsections (b), (c), (m), or (o) of **Section 414** or **Section 1563** of the Internal Revenue Code shall be aggregated.
* **(c) Recapture (Clawback) Tax on Premature Termination:**
  1. **Trigger:** If a taxpayer claims the Safe Harbor exemption under subsection (a) and subsequently terminates or reduces the compensation of the reassigned employee without cause prior to the expiration of the **36-month period**, the previously exempted 15.30 percent tax shall immediately become due and payable.
  2. **Statutory Surcharge:** Any recapture amount under paragraph (1) shall be assessed together with a statutory administrative surcharge of **2.00 percent** (`200 basis points`) over the recaptured tax amount to compensate for trust fund deferral.

#### SEC. 5000H. DEFINITIONS, TAXONOMY OF DISPLACEMENT, AND CLOUD/SAAS IMMUNITY.
* **(a) Automated Asset:** Any artificial intelligence system, autonomous software agent, algorithmic dispatch controller, cyber-physical virtual power plant (`AuraIntel VPP`), or robotic unit deployed in commercial operations.
* **(b) Effective Displaced Baseline Payroll:** The annualized gross wages, salaries, and FICA-taxable compensation paid to the displaced human worker(s) during the 12-month period immediately preceding the displacement event, adjusted proportionally in basis points (`1 to 10,000 bps`) according to the technical species of displacement:
  1. **Direct Replacement (`DirectReplacement`):** 100.00% (`10,000 bps`) of baseline payroll.
  2. **Task Compression (`TaskCompression`):** Exact proportion (`displaced_share_bps`) of headcount reduced across a functional team.
  3. **Wage Deskilling (`WageDeskilling`):** Exact proportion (`wage_reduction_bps`) of compensation reduced due to algorithmic downgrade of role complexity.
  4. **Skill Obsolescence (`SkillObsolescence`):** 100.00% (`10,000 bps`) of baseline payroll when an employee is terminated due to technological shift without an internal reskilling offer.
  5. **Hiring Freeze / Natural Attrition (`HiringFreezeAttrition`):** 0.00% (`0 bps` — Non-Taxable). Unfilled vacancies, voluntary resignations, or retirements do not constitute active displacement.
  6. **Frictional Transition (`FriccionalTransition`):** 0.00% (`0 bps` — Non-Taxable). Operational adjustments not exceeding 90 days.
* **(c) Permanent Displacement Duration Threshold (>90 Days Rule):** A displacement event shall only be deemed structural and subject to Section 5000F if the elimination of the position or reduction in compensation persists for more than **90 consecutive calendar days**.
* **(d) Cloud Computing, Infrastructure Host, and SaaS Developer Liability Immunity:**
  1. **Absolute Developer & Host Immunity:** Under no circumstances shall the creator, architect, software developer, licensor, Cloud Infrastructure provider, or Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) host be treated as the taxpayer or held liable for the tax imposed by Section 5000F by reason of developing, training, hosting, licensing, or expanding automated systems or cloud capacity.
  2. **End-Client Attribution:** Tax liability under this Chapter attaches exclusively to the end-user corporate employer that exercises legal authority over its own payroll and executes the termination or wage reduction of its human personnel.

## SECTION 4. FIDUCIARY TRUST FUND ALLOCATION.
100 percent of the revenues collected under Chapter 56 of the Internal Revenue Code (including any recapture amounts under Section 5000G(c)) shall be segregated via double-entry accounting and deposited immediately into the Treasury of the United States as follows:
1. **75.00 Percent (`7500 basis points`)** to the **Federal Old-Age and Survivors Insurance Trust Fund and Federal Disability Insurance Trust Fund (Social Security)** (Account `2200-FED-TREASURY-SOCIAL-SECURITY`).
2. **25.00 Percent (`2500 basis points`)** to the **Federal Workforce Reskilling and Technological Transition Trust Fund** (Account `2210-FED-WORKFORCE-RESKILLING-FUND`).

## SECTION 5. OFFICIAL 14-DIMENSIONAL TAXONOMY OF AI PROFESSIONAL DISPLACEMENT.
Every automated expansion event audited under this Act is classified across 14 formal dimensions:
1. **Spheres (3):** Cognitive/Intellectual, Physical/Cyber-Physical (`AuraIntel VPP`), Relational/Services.
2. **Orders (3):** Microeconomic (Intra-firm), Mesoeconomic (Supply-Chain/Sectoral), Macroeconomic (Global/National).
3. **Classes (4):** Class I Substitutive, Class II Compressive, Class III Degradative (Deskilling), Class IV Exclusionary (Entry-Barrier).
4. **Families (4):** Ordinary Payroll (FTE), B2B Contractors/Outsourcing, Platform/Gig Economy, Future Workforce Aspirants.
5. **Genera (5):** Direct Active Termination, Indirect Active Downgrade, Passive Attrition, Preventive Hiring Freeze, Competitive Third-Party Closure.
6. **Statutory Categories (3):** Taxable (`Sec. 5000F`), Exempt via Safe Harbor/Immunity (`Sec. 5000G` / `Sec. 5000H(d)`), Non-Subject (`Sec. 5000H(c)`).
7. **Technical Species (6):** `DirectReplacement`, `TaskCompression`, `WageDeskilling`, `SkillObsolescence`, `HiringFreezeAttrition`, `FriccionalTransition`.
8. **AI Varieties (8):** Autonomous Agentic, Multimodal Generative, Real-Time Algorithmic Dispatch, Predictive Analytics, Conversational Interface, Robotic Manipulation, DevSecOps/FinOps Orchestration, Legal-Accounting Scrutiny.
9. **Occupational Groups (4):** Administrative Support, Technical/STEM/Legal, Industrial/Operative, Creative/Editorial.
10. **Organizational Hierarchies (4):** Entry/Base Level, Intermediate Specialist, Middle Management, Tactical Executive.
11. **Socioeconomic Strata (4):** Stratum I Subsistence, Stratum II Standardized Middle, Stratum III High Specialization, Stratum IV IP/System Architecture Owners.
12. **Severity Levels (4):** Level 1 Assistance (<25%), Level 2 Hybridization (25-50%), Level 3 Severe Compression (51-89%), Level 4 Full Autonomy (90-100%).
13. **Reversibility Degrees (4):** Degree 1 Immediately Reassignable (<30d), Degree 2 Reskillable (3-12m), Degree 3 Chronic Sectoral, Degree 4 Irreversible Exit.
14. **Quantitative Ranges (4):** Duration (`0-90d` vs `>=91d`), Safe Harbor (`0-35m` vs `>=36m`), Basis Points (`0-10,000 bps`), Consolidated Gross Receipts (`<=$5M` vs `>$5M USD`).
